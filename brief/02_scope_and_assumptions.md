# Scope and assumptions

Agreed with the client in place of a Q&A session.

- **"Sales are flat"** means total monthly revenue in the second year is about the same as in the first year. We will compare month by month.
- **Period:** Dec 2009 to Dec 2011, about 2 years of orders.
- **Revenue** = quantity x unit price. Cancelled orders and returns are not counted as sales.
- **Currency:** British pounds (GBP), as in the original data.
- **No cost data:** we cannot calculate profit. "Makes us money" means revenue.
- **Best customers:** defined by how recently, how often, and how much they buy (RFM).
- **Success:** the client can read the report in 5 minutes and knows 3 concrete actions to take.
