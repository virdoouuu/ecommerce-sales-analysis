Job Post: Help! My online shop sales stopped growing. Need a data analyst

Posted: Fixed-price | Budget: $250 (open to a fair offer) | Deadline: 10 days | Experience level: Entry to Intermediate

About my business
We run a small online shop selling home and gift items (think decorations, kitchen items, seasonal products). We started about 3 years ago and sell mostly to individual shoppers, plus a few repeat buyers who order in bigger amounts. There are 6 of us. I handle marketing and the orders, and nobody on the team is “a numbers person.”

The problem
For the last year, our sales have been flat. We worked hard, ran some promotions, added new products, but the total money coming in barely moved. I feel like we are working in the dark. I don’t know:

which products really make us money, and which ones just take up space in the warehouse
whether our customers come back to buy again, or buy once and disappear
which countries or regions are worth focusing on
whether we should spend our limited time on getting new customers or on keeping the ones we have

My business partner thinks we should add more products. I think we should push the best sellers harder. We need facts, not opinions.

What data I have
I can export our order history from the shop system as a spreadsheet. It has roughly 2 years of orders: order date, product name and code, quantity, price, customer ID, and country. It is not perfectly clean. Some orders were cancelled, and I know a few entries were typed in by hand and may have mistakes.

What I’m looking for

A short report I can actually read (1 page, no jargon) with what you found and what you recommend I do
A simple dashboard I can open and click around to look at sales by product, time, and country
An Excel file with your results so my team can look at the numbers themselves

Nice to have

Some way to tell who our best customers are
Anything surprising you notice in the data that I didn’t ask about

What I need from you

Please tell me how you will approach this and what you need from me
Experience with e-commerce data is a plus, but I’ll consider anyone who can show me clear work
Please don’t send me a huge technical document. I want to understand it.

To apply: Send a short proposal and any questions you have before starting.
